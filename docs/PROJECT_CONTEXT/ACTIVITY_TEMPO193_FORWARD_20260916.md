# Old-pool activity tempo Paper trial, 2026-09-16

## Observed gap and fixed hypothesis

The current `alpha149_mature_revival_v1` requires a local 30-second trajectory and a pool age of at least six hours. The current user explicitly wants older tokens to remain eligible without spending collection capacity on an arbitrary six-hour gate. Existing snapshot payloads already carry provider-calculated five-minute and one-hour trade counts, five-minute volume and price change, and original-pool liquidity. This trial uses those fields in the same received frame, with no historical re-query or new API call. It is a distinct entry mechanism, not a replacement of `alpha149_mature_revival_v1` or proof of alpha.

The fixed pre-deployment research slice was generic `token_snapshots.id` 878816-928816, 22,081 Dex/Gecko native frames. Among Dex frames, six distinct tokens at least one hour old met the *later frozen* rule below, without selecting from the user's post-hoc winner list. No later generic same-provider/same-pool snapshot fell within 60 seconds of those six first qualifying generic frames. This does **not** prove that the high-priority market-mark or exact-pool safety lanes lacked a next quote; those have different storage and request paths. Gecko's normalized pair had no top-level `txns`, but its contemporaneous raw pool attributes did carry the one-hour counters. A parser fallback now exposes those already acquired fields for this arm. The six historical hits are discovery diagnostics only, never retroactive signals, fills or profitability evidence.

## Implemented definition and boundaries

`activity193_old_pool_tempo_v1` is a new 20U Paper-only account with at most six concurrent positions and a 30-minute maximum hold. It inherits the established parent exit and execution checks. One frame must have ordered `observed_at <= ingested_at <= recorded_at <= decision_at`, age at most 30 seconds and observation no earlier than runtime activation; token, pool and chain must match. The original-pool quote must have a positive price and liquidity at least max(2000U, the shared effective pool floor). Pool age must be at least 3600 seconds, five-minute volume at least 1000U, and five-minute price change nonnegative. Let `N5=B5+S5`, `P55=(B1+S1)-N5`; require `N5>=15`, `S5>=1`, `B5/N5>=0.55`, `P55>=11`, and `11*N5/P55>=3`. Missing, negative, inconsistent or stale inputs are not zero-filled. One runtime-local bounded token/pool signal is issued; standard cohort safety and a next independent observation remain necessary before a Paper BUY. A positive signal alone is not a trade.

The rule is a mechanism hypothesis: activity can accelerate before a large move, but manipulation, delayed reporting, adverse slippage, rug and full writeoff remain real risks. It does not use later ATH, post-hoc popular ranking, user address allow-list or a future candle. Parent exits may still wash out, so result review must include hold time, realized net return, missing exits, pool writeoffs and independent-token counts; a larger signal count is not success. No research schedule or automatic parameter change was added.
