## Research synthesis

External research supports treating this as a market-microstructure/actor-generation problem, not merely a price-pattern problem:

- Chainalysis 2025 market-manipulation work detects suspicious DEX wash-like behavior using repeated near-matched buy/sell cycles: one address buys and sells within ~25 blocks, USD sizes differ by <1%, repeated >=3 times. Its second heuristic clusters controller-funded wallets and looks for near-balanced pool-level buy/sell value. It explicitly warns that behavioral patterns do not prove intent.
- Cong/Li/Tang/Yang, Management Science 2023 / NBER 30783: abnormal trade-size rounding, first-digit/Benford deviations and transaction-size distributions are useful manipulation signals.
- Falk/Tsoukalas/Zhang, Research Policy 2026, "Detecting crypto wash trades via machine learning": adds trade price, round level, trading frequency and inter-trade intervals to distributional features.
- Zwydak et al., Entropy 2026: artificial transaction-count regimes can show sharply higher transaction counts without proportional volume or return fluctuations; autocorrelation, approximate entropy and cross-correlation structure are useful diagnostics.
- SolRugDetector, arXiv:2603.24625 (2026): Solana rug behavior is heavily on-chain/market-manipulation driven and exhibits short lifecycles and organized group behavior.
- "Catching the Rug", arXiv:2608.20271 (2026): first-5-minute Solana features such as tx count, purchase/sale %, unique buyers/sellers, buy/sell count and value ratios, price change and buy/sell price dispersion can predict later rug-like outcomes; forward rolling validation is necessary because of concept drift; XGBoost was stronger than the tested tabular deep models.
- Bitquery 2026 investigation (lower-authority industry evidence): among 339 Solana tokens with high volume generated by <=4 wallets, none later attracted >200 real traders. Treat this only as a hypothesis-supporting observation, not ground truth.
- Official/maintained Pump SDK material exposes `TradeEvent`, `AmmBuyEvent`, `AmmSellEvent` and transaction event decoders. Solana official `logsSubscribe` can subscribe to transactions mentioning a single pubkey per subscription, allowing a bounded per-pool/per-bonding-curve path instead of a global firehose.

### Feature groups

A. Timing / periodicity
- inter-trade interval mean/std/CV
- lag autocorrelation of intervals and trade count
- dominant period / spectral peak ratio
- burstiness and approximate entropy
- phase/grid-lock score (trades repeatedly landing near a fixed cadence)

B. Trade-size regularity
- exact/rounded repeated-size ratio
- size entropy / number of distinct size buckets
- round-number ratio
- first-digit/Benford deviation where sample size supports it
- matched buy-sell amount ratio within short windows

C. Buy/sell sequence structure
- buy/sell alternation rate (ABAB behavior)
- sign run-length distribution / sign entropy
- lag-1 trade-sign autocorrelation
- matched round-trip count and value
- buy-count pressure versus buy-value pressure divergence

D. Actor / wallet breadth where point-in-time observable
- unique buyers / unique sellers
- new-wallet-per-minute rate
- repeat-wallet share
- trader-volume HHI; top1/top3 share
- same-wallet short-horizon buy->sell round trips
- controller/funding-cluster evidence only when independently observable; absence is UNKNOWN, not safe

E. Price-discovery / flow coupling
- tx-count growth versus volume growth
- volume versus absolute return / realized volatility
- buy pressure versus actual quote-vault inflow
- trade count/volume versus executable recovery change
- repeated buy bursts that mechanically restore a dip with low actor breadth (`dip_support_latency` / `support_concentration`)

F. Unwind / cliff-risk precursors
- large-sell share and max sell relative to real quote reserve
- quote-vault slope, base-vault slope, effective-depth slope
- full-position executable-recovery slope
- abrupt switch from recurring buy support to sells
- cessation of a previously strong support cadence
- concentrated-wallet selling / breadth collapse
- route/sellability deterioration

## Exit design motivated by the screenshots: do not equate drawdown with trend death

A fixed peak-drawdown threshold is too crude. A long winner may repeatedly pull back and reclaim before the final cliff. New additive strategies should compare state-dependent exits.

### New strategy candidate A — `ORGANIC_TREND_RUNNER`

Purpose: keep genuine broadening winners longer.

- Entry can reuse an existing broad/flow entry family, but this is a new independent exit behavior.
- Track total executable equity, not pre-fill Dex signal price.
- A pullback enters `RECLAIM_WATCH`, not immediate SELL, when trend continuation remains strong: higher-high/higher-low structure, positive flow, growing/diverse buyers, stable quote depth and stable full-position recovery.
- Normalize drawdown by short-window realized volatility and drawdown velocity; do not use one universal percentage.
- Start a bounded reclaim clock. If the position reclaims a meaningful part of the drawdown/new high while flow/depth remain healthy, continue holding and reset the runner state.
- Exit when reclaim fails and multiple state variables deteriorate: lower high/lower low, buy-value collapse, sell concentration, depth/recovery slope negative, or route quality deteriorates.
- Exact RED/DEAD / catastrophic executable-recovery loss always preempts this logic.

### New strategy candidate B — `PRINCIPAL_RECOVERY_RUNNER`

Purpose: solve the "sell too early vs lose the whole winner" trade-off.

- After a sufficiently large **executable-equity** gain (threshold to be tested, not guessed), sell only enough to recover original debit + modeled costs.
- Let the remaining runner use a much wider state-dependent exit instead of ordinary staged full liquidation.
- This can preserve exposure to a 5x/10x tail while reducing the damage from a later cliff. Use actual amount-specific SELL quotes for the recovery fraction.
- Test this as a distinct strategy/arm, not as a silent modification to old strategies.

### New strategy candidate C — `SYNTHETIC_SUPPORT_SCOUT` (high-risk Paper only initially)

Purpose: test whether manipulation-like support can itself be a tradable short-horizon state rather than automatically rejecting it.

- High `REGULARITY_SCORE` + high `SYNTHETIC_SUPPORT_SCORE` + low organic breadth does not have to be a universal reject.
- Use a smaller independent risk budget / no averaging-up until forward evidence exists.
- While recurring support remains intact and executable depth/recovery are stable, the strategy may continue holding.
- Exit immediately on `SUPPORT_BREAK`: cadence disappears or flips, concentrated sells appear, quote-vault/effective-depth/recovery deteriorate, or sellability degrades. Do not wait for a conventional peak-drawdown trigger.
- This is deliberately behaviorally distinct from the organic runner and from a normal defensive strategy.

The three candidates are examples for the first tranche, not a hard cap. Old 124 remain untouched; new strategies are additive.
